Net contribution means what remains after the direct costs associated with that sale. Zero for usage or setup is a placeholder, not a statement that those charges are waived.

05 / START WITH ONE WORKFLOW

Build a system
you will actually use.

A good first setup is small enough to test and clear enough to repeat.

  1. Choose one clear outcome

    For example: keep every agreed follow-up visible, or organize contacts into a simple pipeline.

  2. Confirm your account and tools

    Check which features are available, connected, and included in your plan.

  3. Import a small, reviewed list

    Map the fields, check duplicates, and keep the relationship context intact.

  4. Configure and test one workflow

    Use your own test contact. Check timing, content, routing, and how to pause it.

  5. Review the real activity

    Look at responses and completed next steps. Adjust the system to what you learn.
