BUSINESS CLIENTS / TWO MORE OPENERS

Talk about their business.
Make the question easy to answer.

Use these for owners and finance leaders. Adapt the detail and the service to the person.

OWNER / CEO · THE BIG PICTURE

Hey [First Name] — I noticed [Genuine detail] at [Company]. I work with business owners who want a clearer view of operating costs, employee benefits, and potential tax-credit opportunities. Would you be opposed to me sending over a quick overview?

CFO / FINANCE · A FOCUSED QUESTION

Hi [First Name] — when did [Company] last review whether its current activities might qualify for R&D tax credits? If a fresh look would be useful, I can share an overview of the review process and the information a qualified specialist would need. Would that be relevant to your priorities right now?

Keep claims specific to what you can substantiate. A conversation or an overview does not establish tax-credit eligibility, savings, or financial results.

in◎✦↗+
A SHARED INTEREST

A better opening
than a cold pitch.

Notice a thoughtful post. Add a useful perspective. Ask a relevant question. Let familiarity grow.

  1. 1

    Find a relevant community.

    Search by industry, business interest, or professional specialty.

  2. 2

    Read before you join.

    Look for recent conversations and clear rules. Request membership; approval is up to the group.

  3. 3

    Contribute something useful.

    Answer a question, share a helpful idea, or add a relevant resource. Follow the group’s rules.

  4. 4

    Continue a real conversation.

    A shared-group message request may be available. Recipient settings and LinkedIn limits apply; people can decline.
